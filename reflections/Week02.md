Task 1 — Model selection worksheet

Brief A - Aegis Flight-Control Software Update

Chosen Model - Waterfall
Requirement stability - requirements are clearly stated and unlikely to change 
risk/safety - the risk is high because if something goes wrong it could endanger lives. The waterfall method provides structured stages and alot of testing and documentation
timeline - The longer structured process is best because there is little pressure to get it out quickly.
customer involvement - little involvement is needed because the requirements are clearly stated. 
Risk of model - if requirements change through the project then it can be difficult and expensive to make the changes.
