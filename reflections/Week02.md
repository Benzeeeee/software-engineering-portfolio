Task 1 — Model selection worksheet
Brief A - Aegis Flight-Control Software Update
Chosen Model - Waterfall

Requirement stability - requirements are clearly stated and unlikely to change 

risk/safety - the risk is high because if something goes wrong it could endanger lives. The waterfall method provides structured stages and alot of testing and documentation

timeline - The longer structured process is best because there is little pressure to get it out quickly.

customer involvement - little involvement is needed because the requirements are clearly stated.

Risk of model - if requirements change through the project then it can be difficult and expensive to make the changes.

Brief B -  Riverside Bakery website
Chosen Model: RAD(Rapid Application Development)

Requirement Stability - requirements are liekly to change especially the frontend of the website.

Risk/Safety: Very low risk with no safety or reglatory concerns 

Timeline: Short 3 week deadline making rapid development suitable.

Customer Involvement: High. the owner can regularly review prototypes and give feedback.

Risk of model: Frequent changes from the owner could cause scope creep aand make the three week deadline difficult to meet.

Brief C - CampusCircle Community App
Chosen model: Incremental

Requirement stability: Requirements are expected to change based on student feedback.

Risk/safety: Low Risk, although user dara and privacy still need consideration.

Timeline: They need a usable version within one month, so core features can be released first.

Customer involvement: High. Real students can test early versions and provide feedback for later increments.

Risk of model: Constant changes between increments could make the overall system harder to maintain if they are not managed properly.
